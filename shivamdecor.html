Our Story

Built on Trust,
Driven by Design

Shivam Decor is a Mumbai-based premium interior design and execution firm with over 8 years of expertise. We operate out of Goregaon East and serve clients across Mumbai, the MMR region, and all of India.

Our approach combines architectural sensibility with hands-on craftsmanship. Every project — from a compact apartment to a large commercial complex — receives the same level of dedication, precision, and creative vision. We handle everything: design, carpentry, tiling, painting, styling, and turnkey delivery.

Premium Quality

Only the finest materials, hardware, and finishes — sourced from trusted suppliers and installed by skilled craftsmen.

On-Time Delivery

We respect your timelines. Our project management ensures milestones are met and deadlines honoured.

Transparent Pricing

No hidden costs. Detailed quotations and clear billing so you're always in control of your investment.

End-to-End Service

From first concept sketch to final walk-through, we manage every aspect of your project under one roof.
