Our Story

Built on Trust,
Driven by Design

Shivam Decor is a Mumbai-based premium interior design and execution studio with over 8 years of expertise. Operating out of Goregaon East, we serve clients across Mumbai, the MMR region, and pan-India.

Our approach fuses architectural thinking with hands-on craftsmanship. Whether a compact apartment or a large commercial complex — every project receives the same dedication, precision and creative excellence. Carpentry, tiling, painting, styling, turnkey — all under one roof.

Premium Quality

Finest materials, hardware and finishes sourced from trusted suppliers.

On-Time Delivery

Project milestones met on schedule — no surprises, no delays.

Transparent Pricing

Clear quotations, no hidden costs — full control over your budget.

End-to-End Service

From first sketch to final handover — completely managed by us.
